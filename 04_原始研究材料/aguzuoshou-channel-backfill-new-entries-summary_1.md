## 2. Condensed Frameworks

### 2.1 Beijing Chaojia First-Board Defense

Source: `MTyWMStyUZE`

Core idea: 北京炒家的首板系统不是单点买入技巧，而是用不买清单、分仓、机械卖出、亏损降速和对量化环境的理解，把首板做成长期均值游戏。

Reusable rules:

- Before entry, exclude `2元以下`, ST/problem stocks, suspected dealer stocks, poor-liquidity names, and low-recognition edge cases.
- Use about `10%` as a single-position unit, not as a required minimum.
- During losing streaks, reduce size and frequency first.
- For possible weak-to-strong names, observe the first `5 minutes` before panic-selling.
- Treat broken boards as information; in quant-heavy environments, separate mechanical selling from true thesis failure.

Main misuse:

- Copying high-frequency first-board trading with full capital.
- Treating every broken board as a repair opportunity.
- Using the 5-minute observation rule as an excuse to ignore invalid exits.

### 2.2 Abnormal-Movement Liquidity Premium

Source: `8zZebULgis4`

Core idea: `卡异动` itself is not the edge. The edge is recognizing whether supervision windows, incremental money, sector turnover share, and theme consensus still support a liquidity premium.

Reusable rules:

- Track abnormal-movement windows such as `3天20%`, `10天100%`, and `30天200%` as context, not as standalone buy signals.
- Confirm whether market-wide turnover and sector turnover can support the theme.
- Treat a sector consuming more than roughly `20%` of total turnover as a sign of major liquidity focus, but not as a universal threshold.
- If total turnover shrinks materially, assume the sector carrying capacity is weakening.

Main misuse:

- Believing carding supervision rules is the profit source.
- Ignoring market-wide turnover shrinkage.
- Staying in late-phase themes because early participants made easy money.

### 2.3 Three Trader Types And Wudao

Source: `p67oBV0PU9g`

Core idea: 悟道不是找到一个必胜买点，而是理解自己的交易阶段、胜率/赔率/频率组合、账户承受力和心理反馈机制。

Reusable rules:

- Do not go all-in just because a setup has high win rate.
- Review each model by win rate, payoff ratio, frequency, and maximum drawdown.
- Separate dopamine-driven excitement from endorphin-like stable execution.
- Use position sizing to survive long enough for the model edge to appear.

Main misuse:

- Using "wudao" as a reason to increase size before having records.
- Confusing short-term excitement with stable edge.
- Ignoring frequency: a high-payoff setup may still be hard to compound if it appears too rarely.

### 2.4 First-Principles Certainty

Source: `6Sl8pPnp7jQ`

Core idea: 稳定盈利来自只赚确定的钱：逻辑、信号、风险边界三者同时成立，才允许交易。

Reusable rules:

- Require a clear catalyst or logic: policy support, earnings growth, or sector linkage.
- Require technical confirmation such as 5-day/10-day moving averages turning up and spreading.
- Set the stop-loss before buying; NotebookLM extracted about `5%` as a hard loss boundary.
- Cap trading frequency around `1-2` trades per day at most.

Main misuse:

- Buying before the logic is clear.
- Treating moving-average alignment as enough by itself.
- Trading more because no clear opportunity appears.

### 2.5 Losing-Period Strong-Stock Checklist

Source: `qo62do2SSQI`

Core idea: 亏损期不要在弱势票里找安慰，要用强势股基因、5日线纪律、早盘强度和空仓意识保护本金。

Reusable rules:

- Candidate stock should have had a limit-up within the last `20` trading days.
- Prefer `红肥绿瘦`: large/full positive candles and smaller/lighter negative candles.
- Treat the 5-day line as a holding boundary.
- Watch whether expected board strength appears before `10:00`.
- Treat two unrepaired negative candles as exit evidence.

Main misuse:

- Averaging down in weakness and calling it pullback buying.
- Buying unclear sideways movement.
- Mistaking cash for missing out.

### 2.6 Three Compasses Over Technical Overload

Source: `ylvzP6TrwDA`

Core idea: 技术越学越多容易陷入细节。全职短线真正要看的三根指南针是板块资金流向、政策风口、市场情绪；技术只用于止损和破位识别。

Reusable rules:

- Use technical analysis mainly to identify invalidation and risk.
- Spend most observation time on sector capital flow, policy direction, and market sentiment.
- In unclear markets, observation is a valid position.
- Avoid overfitting indicators that are already too public.

Main misuse:

- Adding indicators when the real problem is poor market selection.
- Treating every technical signal as independent of sector and emotion cycle.
- Forcing trades during the `95%` observation time.

### 2.7 Bad Habits That Eat Capital

Source: `Ppvh-ujhXtY`

Core idea: 很多亏损不是模式问题，而是坏习惯持续吞本金：补仓死扛、交易记录缺失、零碎仓位、亏损后急着回本。

Reusable rules:

- Print and review trade records.
- Stay out for a period, even up to one month, when the account is out of control.
- Use a zero-based mindset: do not try to "win back" a prior loss from the same stock.
- Stop adding into stair-step declines.
- Avoid scattered tiny positions that dilute attention and discipline.

Main misuse:

- Treating review as emotional regret rather than data collection.
- Calling every losing hold "long-term logic."
- Increasing trade count after drawdown.

### 2.8 Trend Mainline Compounding

Source: `U0YAkmx1CD8`

Core idea: 开窍的关键是看势和拥抱趋势，用 `主流题材 + 强势股 + 分歧买入 + 一致卖出 + 绝不大亏` 做复利。

Reusable rules:

- Focus on the current mainline theme.
- Inside the theme, prefer the strongest target, not the cheaper laggard.
- Filter for stocks that had a limit-up in the last `10` trading days.
- Filter for volume-backed box breakout within the last `1` month.
- Buy disagreement after strength appears: pullback that holds the `10日均线` or box lower edge.
- Treat `15%-20%` as a review/target zone, not a guaranteed return.

Main misuse:

- Calling a downtrend pullback "disagreement."
- Drawing the box after losing money to justify holding.
- Buying laggards because leaders look too high.

### 2.9 Three Hurdles: Group Behavior And Model Failure

Source: `3yJcadKD3ec`

Core idea: 短线悟道的三道坎，是把交易从看技术形态推进到理解群体行为、模式失效条件和资金结构平衡。

Reusable rules:

- When reviewing a model, include historical market environment, sector ladder, competing themes, and capital context.
- Write down where the model fails before using it.
- High turnover and popularity can be safety in a crazy market, but instability in a late-stage market.
- The `5日线` is useful because it proxies whether group agreement remains intact.
- Watch whether a new mainline is pulling capital away from the old leader.
- Treat sudden attention dispersion or uncontrolled volume expansion as danger signs.

Main misuse:

- Copying the form of a model while missing its environment.
- Holding a former leader after attention has shifted.
- Thinking high volume is automatically bullish.

## 3. Cross-Entry Rule Synthesis

### Selection Filters

- Prefer current mainline themes and high-recognition leaders.
- Exclude ST/problem names, suspected dealer stocks, very low-price names, poor liquidity, and unclear downtrends.
- Strong-stock filters repeated across entries:
  - Recent limit-up within `10-20` trading days.
  - Volume-backed box breakout within about `1` month.
  - Red-fat-green-thin candle structure.
  - Ability to hold the 5-day or 10-day moving average in pullbacks.

### Entry Rules

- Logic before signal: policy, sector, earnings, capital flow, or theme consensus must explain demand.
- Signal after logic: breakout, moving-average confirmation, board strength, or strong-stock pullback.
- Buy disagreement only after strength is already proven.
- Do not use cheap price, laggard status, or emotional missed-out pain as entry reasons.

### Exit Rules

- Predefine the invalidation level before entry.
- Exit when the 5-day line, 10-day line, box lower edge, or planned stop is broken according to the chosen model.
- Sell crowded agreement/acceleration rather than waiting until everyone is optimistic and liquidity disappears.
- Treat two unrepaired negative candles, failed early board strength, attention dispersion, or market-wide liquidity shrinkage as danger signals.

### Position And Frequency

- Use position size as a survival tool.
- Cut size and frequency after losing streaks.
- One-tenth position appears as a practical Beijing Chaojia-style unit, but should be scaled to account size and experience.
- Low-frequency selectivity appears repeatedly: no clear mainline, no trade.

### Review System

Each trade should be reviewed through these questions:

1. What mainline or capital logic supported the trade?
2. Was this the strongest target or just a cheaper substitute?
3. What exact signal confirmed entry?
4. What exact condition invalidated the trade?
5. Did I buy disagreement after strength, or weakness in a downtrend?
6. Did market liquidity and sector focus still support the setup?
7. Did I reduce size after losses?
8. Did I print and classify the trade record?
9. Was my mistake technical, psychological, or environmental?
10. Is this model still effective in the current market ecology?

## 4. Key Contradictions To Preserve

- High volume can be confirmation in a trend breakout, but danger in a holding-together/late-stage leader structure.
- High win rate does not justify full position; position sizing depends on payoff ratio, frequency, and drawdown.
- A broken board can be failure, or quant-era mechanical disruption; context decides.
- Technicals are useful for invalidation, but dangerous when used as a standalone trading reason.
- Strong leaders are safer than laggards only while capital focus remains; once attention shifts, yesterday's leader can become a trap.
